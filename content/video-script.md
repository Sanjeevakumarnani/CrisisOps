# CrisisOps demo script

**0:00–0:25 — Intro**  
Emergency response produces huge amounts of operational information. CrisisOps combines live public hazard intelligence with human field reports and persistent memory so earlier experience is available before the next decision.

**0:25–0:55 — Real data first**  
Show the live intelligence panel. Point out current weather, India Meteorological Department CAP alerts, USGS earthquake data and GDACS events. Explain the key rule: these are external public signals with visible source and timestamp, not invented field status.

**0:55–1:25 — Capture and retain**  
Show the field-report form. Enter a realistic operational report with location, urgency, needs, resources, transport constraints and an outcome. Explain that field reports are the operational ground-truth layer and are retained as structured memory.

**1:25–1:50 — Put live signals into memory**  
Click **Retain in Hindsight**. Show the confirmation. Explain that current public signals can now become persistent memory with stable identifiers, source tags and timestamps.

**1:50–2:40 — Recall**  
Open Memory Review and submit a response plan. Show the generic baseline beside the memory-backed review. Point out recalled history, evidence, recurring patterns, resource conflicts and transport constraints.

**2:40–3:20 — Reflect**  
Explain that Hindsight is doing more than keyword search: retained memories are recalled and then reflected into a structured review. The system surfaces what previous experience says while keeping uncertainty visible.

**3:20–3:50 — Outcome and learning**  
Add a follow-up event with the actual outcome. Explain that the outcome becomes part of future memory. A later response plan can retrieve the earlier situation and its result.

**3:50–4:10 — Safety and takeaway**  
CrisisOps does not autonomously dispatch responders, alter routes or claim that a person is safe without evidence. Real public feeds provide current context, field reports provide operational truth, and Hindsight connects today's decision with yesterday's experience.
