# CrisisOps — demo video script

**0:00–0:20 — The problem**
During a flood, teams can have the current information but still lose the operational lesson from the previous incident. CrisisOps is designed to remember the experience, not just display the moment.

**0:20–0:50 — Retain Incident A**
Click **Seed Incident A + B**. Point out that Incident A is marked `SYNTHETIC`: North Ward flooding, 120 people affected, water and medical-support needs, bridge closed, road delivery attempted, and the route became inaccessible. Show the retained Hindsight document ID.

**0:50–1:30 — Recall + Reflect on Incident B**
Submit the second scenario: North Ward flooding again, 150 people affected, 2 boats and 1 truck, Bridge 4 closed, with a canal route proposed. The left side is the memory-free baseline; the right side is the Hindsight-informed review. Show the recalled incident and the reflected transport lesson.

**1:30–2:00 — Make memory visible**
Point to the recalled history, recurring patterns, transport constraints and recommended human checks. Explain that Hindsight Recall brings relevant prior experience into context and Reflect synthesizes that experience into structured decision support.

**2:00–2:35 — Living Memory**
Open the `What keeps breaking?`, `What actually worked`, `Where capacity is fragile`, and `Institutional memory` lenses. These are labelled `HINDSIGHT SYNTHESIS`, while the underlying records retain their source type.

**2:35–3:10 — Add the learning incident**
Run the learning-curve action. Incident C records that the canal route remained usable and boat staging reduced road dependence. Reflect on the same plan again and show the new retained experience available to future reviews.

**3:10–3:40 — Real public intelligence**
Show the live intelligence panel: Open-Meteo weather, IMD CAP, USGS earthquakes and GDACS. Every external item is marked `REAL` with source and retrieval time. Explain that this is public intelligence, not verified field status.

**3:40–4:00 — Close**
The operator remains in control: CrisisOps does not autonomously dispatch responders or alter routes. The product separates `REAL` public intelligence, `OPERATOR INPUT`, and `SYNTHETIC` demo data, while Hindsight connects today's plan to yesterday's operational experience.
